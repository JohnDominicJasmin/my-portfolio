## Full scan coverage (15 pages)

Scanned: /, /cv, /partners, /partners/thanks, /thanks, /hvac, /restoration, /privacy, and all 7 /work pages.

Per page, every one of these came back clean: zero em dashes, zero filler phrases, zero generic CTAs, exactly one H1, zero dead links, zero horizontal overflow at 375px, zero contrast failures at WCAG AA, and a title and meta description on every page. Images on the work pages all carry alt text, with the header portrait correctly marked decorative.

Only two pages carry anything at all: the buzzwords in finding 12.

## Copy pass (skill: antislop-copywriting)

Scanned /, /cv, /partners, /hvac and /restoration.

- **Zero buzzwords** from R-16 and the empty-AI-vocabulary list: no seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, unlock, elevate, empower, robust, leverage, streamline, world-class, industry-leading.
- **Zero generic CTAs.** Every button names its action: "Book a free Missed-Call Leak Check", "Hear it work", "Show me what to fix first", "Watch it run", "Apply to partner", "Send it".
- **No filler phrases, no stacked hedging, no chatbot closers, no signposting openers.**
- **No rhythm tells:** no forced rule of three, no aphorism formulas, no staccato runs. The one "not just" is an ordinary contrast inside a fit list ("running and supported, not just built"), not the "it's not just X, it's Y" formula.
- **Sentence rhythm is human:** 153 sentences on the home page, average 16.6 words, median 13, with real short and long variation.
- **Emphasis is restrained:** 2 bold tags on the home page, 3 on /cv, 0 on /partners. Quotation marks appear only around real client testimonials.
- **Voice is present and specific:** "Most builds break in the first busy week. That lands on you." No sterile default.

## Passes (evidence)

- **Contrast (R-25):** every text and background pairing on /, /cv and /partners was computed with the WCAG formula. Zero failures at 4.5:1 normal and 3:1 large. The one reported failure was a false positive on a `<script>` text node.
- **Keyboard and focus (R-32):** 12 `:focus-visible` rules, all a 2px amber outline with an offset, including a dark-band variant. No `outline: none` anywhere in the CSS.
- **Dead controls (R-26, R-24):** 84 interactive elements on the home page, zero dead links, zero placeholder hrefs. Every sitemap URL returns 200.
- **Honest content (R-17, R-18, R-36, R-38):** no fabricated statistics, no invented testimonials, demo cases labelled "Demo", client quotes are real and attributed.
- **Copy (R-02):** zero em dashes and zero decorative emoji across every page.
- **Slop shapes (R-05, R-01, R-10, R-13):** no bento mosaic, no fake terminal, no three-column pricing, no four-column template footer, no background grid, no glassmorphism (zero `backdrop-filter` in use), only 4 box-shadows on the whole home page, and no gradient in the CSS at all.
- **Palette (R-29):** ink `rgb(24,22,20)`, cream `rgb(242,239,233)`, teal `rgb(9,90,83)` plus the amber accent. Within the 2-3 core colors plus one accent limit.
- **Mobile (R-03):** no horizontal overflow at 375px or 768px; forms use real labels on every field; the Netlify honeypot is correctly parked off-screen at -9999px.
- **States (R-27):** the chat widget and the audit quiz both carry loading, error and empty handling. The leak calculator is synchronous, so it needs none.
